# CAPM 100-Question Scenario Practice Exam

### Question 1 (Initiating)

**A project manager is assigned to a new digital transformation effort. Before planning begins, the executive sponsor presents a document detailing market demand, cost-benefit analysis, and strategic alignment to justify why the project is worth the investment. Which input to Develop Project Charter is the PM reviewing?**

A. Benefits Management Plan
B. Business Case
C. Project Scope Statement
D. Assumption Log

---

### Question 2 (Initiating)

**During a project kick-off meeting, a PM notes that local municipal permits are assumed to be approved within 10 days, but safety regulations mandate that construction cannot occur after 8 PM. In which output of Develop Project Charter should these factors be logged?**

A. Issue Log
B. Risk Register
C. Assumption Log
D. Requirements Traceability Matrix

### Question 3 (Initiating)

**A project manager is conducting stakeholder analysis for a new factory build. She identifies a senior vice president who holds final budget approval authority but expresses very low interest in daily construction activities. According to the Power/Interest Grid, how should this stakeholder be managed?**

A. Manage Closely
B. Keep Satisfied
C. Keep Informed
D. Monitor

### Question 4 (Initiating)
